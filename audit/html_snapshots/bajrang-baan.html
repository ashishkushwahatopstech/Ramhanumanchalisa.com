सुरक्षा कवच • Protection Shield

Shree Bajrang Baan

The Bajrang Baan is an arrow (baan) of protection dedicated to Lord Hanuman. Devotees chant it to dispel fear, remove negative influences, and overcome severe obstacles.

॥🏹॥
Opening Couplets (दोहा)

निश्चय प्रेम प्रतीति ते, विनय करैं सनमान। तेहि के कारज सकल शुभ, सिद्ध करैं हनुमान॥

niścaya prema pratīti te, vinaya karaiṁ sanamāna। tehi ke kāraja sakala śubha, siddha karaiṁ hanumāna॥

With assured love and belief in you, and with humility, I pay respect. May Hanuman accomplish all auspicious tasks.

॥ श्री बजरंग बाण पाठ ॥

The Bajrang Baan Verses

॥ बाण चौपाई 2 ॥ Verse 2

जय हनुमन्त सन्त हितकारी। सुनि लीजै प्रभु अरज हमारी॥

jaya hanumanta santa hitakārī। suni lījai prabhu araja hamārī॥

Victory to Hanuman, the benefactor of saints. Listen and accept our petition, O Lord.

This verse is a humble invocation to Lord Hanuman, who is known to be the benefactor of saints, to listen to our prayers and grant us victory in our endeavours.

॥ बाण चौपाई 3 ॥ Verse 3

जन के काज विलम्ब न कीजै। आतुर दौरि महा सुख दीजै॥

jana ke kāja vilamba na kījai। ātura dauri mahā sukha dījai॥

Do not delay in the work of the people, run to the distressed and give great happiness.

The verse is a call for divine intervention, asking for immediate assistance and relief for those in distress, symbolizing the power of faith in times of hardship.

॥ बाण चौपाई 4 ॥ Verse 4

जैसे कूदि सिन्धु वहि पारा। सुरसा बदन पैठि विस्तारा॥

jaise kūdi sindhu vahi pārā। surasā badan paiṭhi vistārā॥

Like jumping across the ocean, Surasa's body expanded.

This verse metaphorically describes overcoming challenges and expanding one's abilities, as Hanuman did when he leapt across the ocean and faced the mythical creature Surasa.

॥ बाण चौपाई 5 ॥ Verse 5

आगे जाय लंकिनी रोका। मारेहु लात गई सुर लोका॥

āge jāya laṅkinī rokā। mārehu lāta gaī sura lokā॥

Going ahead, Lankini stops (him). Striking a kick, (she) goes to the world of gods.

The verse signifies the power of devotion and righteousness, embodied by Hanuman, overcoming obstacles, represented by Lankini.

॥ बाण चौपाई 6 ॥ Verse 6

जाय विभीषण को सुख दीन्हा। सीता निरखि परम पद लीन्हा॥

Jāya vibhīṣaṇa ko sukha dīnhā। Sītā nirakhi parama pada līnhā॥

Went to Vibhishan and gave happiness. Seeing Sita, attained the supreme position.

The verse implies that by serving the righteous and the virtuous, one can attain the highest position of spiritual enlightenment.

॥ बाण चौपाई 7 ॥ Verse 7

बाग उजारि सिन्धु महं बोरा। अति आतुर यम कातर तोरा॥

bāga ujāri sindhu mahaṁ borā। ati ātura yama kātara torā॥

Destroyer of gardens, causing great distress in the ocean, extremely distressed is Yama, terrified by you.

The verse metaphorically describes Hanuman's immense power and fearlessness, capable of causing distress even to the god of death, Yama.

॥ बाण चौपाई 8 ॥ Verse 8

अक्षय कुमार को मारि संहारा। लूम लपेटि लंक को जारा॥

akṣaya kumāra ko māri sanhārā। lūma lapeṭi laṅka ko jārā॥

Killed the indestructible son, destroyed. Wrapped in his tail, set Lanka on fire.

The verse signifies Hanuman's immense power and fearlessness, as he defeats the indestructible son (Akshay Kumar) of Ravana and sets Lanka on fire.

॥ बाण चौपाई 9 ॥ Verse 9

लाह समान लंक जरि गई। जय जय धुनि सुर पुर में भई॥

lāha samāna lanka jari gaī। jaya jaya dhuni sura pura mēṁ bhaī॥

Lanka, similar to a flame, was burned and is now gone. The sound of victory resounds in the city of the gods.

This verse symbolizes the triumph of good over evil, represented by the burning of Lanka. The sound of victory in the city of the gods signifies divine approval and celebration.

॥ बाण चौपाई 10 ॥ Verse 10

अब विलंब केहि कारण स्वामी। कृपा करहु प्रभु अन्तर्यामी॥

ab vilamba kehi kāraṇa svāmī। kṛpā karahu prabhu antaryāmī॥

Why is there a delay now, Lord? Show your grace, O inner controller.

The verse is a call for divine intervention, expressing urgency and seeking immediate action from the divine power that controls everything from within.

॥ बाण चौपाई 11 ॥ Verse 11

जय जय लक्ष्मण प्राण के दाता। आतुर होई दुःख करहु निपाता॥

jaya jaya lakṣmaṇa prāṇa ke dātā। ātura hoī duḥkha karahu nipātā॥

Victory to Lakshman, the giver of life. Destroy the sorrow when distressed.

This verse is a call for spiritual strength and resilience, seeking divine intervention from Lakshman to alleviate suffering and distress.

॥ बाण चौपाई 12 ॥ Verse 12

जय गिरधर जय जय सुख सागर। सुर समूह समरथ भट नागर॥

jaya giridhara jaya jaya sukha sāgara। sura samūha samaratha bhaṭa nāgara॥

Victory to Giridhara, victory, victory to the ocean of happiness. The group of gods is capable, the soldier of the city.

The verse praises Lord Krishna as the embodiment of joy and happiness. It signifies that all the gods and their groups are capable soldiers serving in the divine city of the Lord.

॥ बाण चौपाई 13 ॥ Verse 13

ॐ हनु-हनु-हनु हनुमंत हठीले। बैरिहिं मारू बज्र सम कीलें॥

oṁ hanu-hanu-hanu hanumant haṭhīle। bairihiṁ mārū bajra sam kīle॥

O Hanuman, the stubborn one, kill the enemies like nails are hit by a thunderbolt.

The verse is a call to Hanuman's strength and resolve, asking him to destroy obstacles and enemies with the force of a thunderbolt.

॥ बाण चौपाई 14 ॥ Verse 14

गदा बज्र तें बैरिहिं मारौ। महाराज निज दास उबारौ॥

gadā bajra teṁ bairihiṁ mārau। mahārāj nij dās ubārau॥

With mace and thunderbolt, slay the enemies. O Great King, save your servant.

The verse symbolizes the divine intervention of the Almighty in protecting his devotees from evil forces.

॥ बाण चौपाई 15 ॥ Verse 15

सुनि हुंकार हुंकार दें धावो। बज्र गदा हनु विलम्ब न लावो॥

suni huṃkāra huṃkāra deṃ dhāvo। bajra gadā hanu vilamba na lāvo॥

Listen to the roar and run, Hanuman. Do not delay in wielding your thunderbolt mace.

The verse is a call for Hanuman to act swiftly and decisively, using his divine power to overcome obstacles.

॥ बाण चौपाई 16 ॥ Verse 16

ॐ ह्रीं ह्रीं ह्रीं हनुमंत कपीसा। ॐ हुँ हुँ हुँ हनु अरि उर शीसा॥

oṃ hrīṃ hrīṃ hrīṃ hanumant kapīśa। oṃ huṃ huṃ huṃ hanu ari ura śīśa॥

Om Hrim Hrim Hrim Hanuman, the Monkey Chief. Om Hum Hum Hum, crush the enemies at their chest and head.

The verse invokes the divine power of Hanuman, symbolizing strength and courage to overcome obstacles and enemies, both external and internal.

॥ बाण चौपाई 17 ॥ Verse 17

सत्य होहु हरि सत्य पाय कें। राम दूत धरू मारू धाई कें॥

satya hohu hari satya pāya keṁ। rāma dūta dharū mārū dhāī keṁ॥

Become the truth, O Hari, obtain the truth. O messenger of Rama, hold and kill the running evil.

The verse is a call to Lord Hanuman to embody the ultimate truth and to destroy the evil forces that are running rampant.

॥ बाण चौपाई 18 ॥ Verse 18

जय हनुमन्त अनंत अगाधा। दुःख पावत जन केहि अपराधा॥

jaya hanumanta ananta agādhā। duḥkha pāvata jana kehi aparādhā॥

Victory to Hanuman, the infinite and unfathomable. People receive sorrow due to their own offenses.

The verse emphasizes the infinite power of Hanuman and suggests that human suffering is often a result of one's own wrongdoings.

॥ बाण चौपाई 19 ॥ Verse 19

पूजा जप तप नेम अचारा। नहिं जानत हैं दास तुम्हारा॥

pūjā japa tapa nema acārā। nahiṁ jānat haiṁ dāsa tumhārā॥

Worship, chanting, austerity, rule, conduct, your servant does not know.

The verse conveys the humility of the devotee who admits his ignorance about the formalities of spiritual practices, emphasizing his complete surrender to the divine.

॥ बाण चौपाई 20 ॥ Verse 20

वन उपवन जल-थल गृह माहीं। तुम्हरे बल हम डरपत नाहीं॥

vana upavana jala-thala gr̥ha māhīṁ। tumhare bala hama ḍarapata nāhīṁ॥

In the forest, garden, water, land, and home, we are not afraid because of your power.

The verse signifies that with the divine power of Lord Hanuman, one can remain fearless in all aspects of life, whether in nature or at home.

॥ बाण चौपाई 21 ॥ Verse 21

पाँय परौं कर जोरि मनावों। अपने काज लागि गुण गावों॥

pā̃ya parauṁ kara jori manāvoṁ। apane kāja lāgi guṇa gāvoṁ॥

I place my hands on my feet and plead, I sing your virtues for my work.

The verse signifies the humility and devotion of the speaker, who is willing to humble themselves and sing praises of the divine for the fulfillment of their tasks.

॥ बाण चौपाई 22 ॥ Verse 22

जय अंजनी कुमार बलवन्ता। शंकर स्वयं वीर हनुमंता॥

jaya aṁjanī kumāra balavantā। śaṅkara svayaṁ vīra hanumantā॥

Victory to the mighty son of Anjani, Lord Shiva himself, the brave Hanuman.

The verse is a salutation to Hanuman, recognizing him as an incarnation of Lord Shiva, and praising his strength and bravery.

॥ बाण चौपाई 23 ॥ Verse 23

बदन कराल दुष्ट कुल घालक। भूत पिशाच प्रेत उर शालक॥

badana karāla duṣṭa kula ghālaka। bhūta piśāca preta ura śālaka॥

The fierce-bodied destroyer of the evil clan, the piercer of the hearts of spirits, demons, and ghosts.

The verse signifies the power of the divine to vanquish all forms of evil and negativity, symbolized by spirits, demons, and ghosts.

॥ बाण चौपाई 24 ॥ Verse 24

भूत प्रेत पिशाच निशाचर। अग्नि बैताल वीर मारी मर॥

bhūta preta piśāca niśācara। agni baitāla vīra mārī mar॥

Ghosts, spirits, demons, night wanderers, fire spirits, vampire-like spirits, brave ones, be killed and die.

This verse is a powerful invocation to eliminate all forms of negative and harmful energies, entities, and influences that may be affecting the devotee's life.

॥ बाण चौपाई 25 ॥ Verse 25

इन्हहिं मारू, तोंहि शपथ राम की। राखु नाथ मर्यादा नाम की॥

inhahiṁ mārū, toṁhi śapath rāma kī। rākhu nātha maryaādā nāma kī॥

"I will kill these, this is my oath to you, O Rama. Protect the dignity of your name, O Lord."

The verse signifies Hanuman's unwavering devotion and commitment to Lord Rama. It also reflects his readiness to fight against evil to uphold the dignity of Rama's name.

॥ बाण चौपाई 26 ॥ Verse 26

जनक सुता पति दास कहावो। ताकी शपथ विलम्ब न लावो॥

janaka sutā pati dāsa kahāvo। tākī śapath vilamb na lāvo॥

I am known as the servant of Sita's husband, I do not delay in fulfilling his oath.

This verse expresses the speaker's devotion and commitment to Lord Rama, and his readiness to act without delay in fulfilling his duties.

॥ बाण चौपाई 27 ॥ Verse 27

जय जय जय ध्वनि होत अकाशा। सुमिरत होत सुसह दुःख नाशा॥

jaya jaya jaya dhvani hota ākāśā। sumirata hota susaha duḥkha nāśā॥

The victory chant resonates in the sky. Remembering it easily destroys sorrow.

The verse emphasizes the power of positive affirmation and spiritual remembrance in overcoming sorrow and hardship.

॥ बाण चौपाई 28 ॥ Verse 28

उठु-उठु चल तोहि राम दुहाई। पाँय परौं कर जोरि मनाई॥

Uṭhu-uṭhu cala tohi rāma duhāī। Pāṅya parauṁ kara jori manāī॥

Arise, arise and move, I plead in the name of Rama. I place my joined hands on my feet in request.

This verse signifies the devotee's deep longing for the divine intervention of Lord Hanuman, invoked with utmost humility and respect.

॥ बाण चौपाई 29 ॥ Verse 29

ॐ चं चं चं चं चपल चलन्ता। ॐ हनु हनु हनु हनु हनुमंता॥

ॐ चं चं चं चं चपल चलन्ता। ॐ हनु हनु हनु हनु हनुमंता॥

Om, the agile one moves. Om, Hanuman.

The verse invokes the swift and agile energy of Hanuman, encouraging the chanter to embody these qualities.

॥ बाण चौपाई 30 ॥ Verse 30

ॐ हं हं हांक देत कपि चंचल। ॐ सं सं सहम पराने खल दल॥

ॐ हं हं हांक देत कपि चंचल। ॐ सं सं सहम पराने खल दल॥

Om, the restless monkey (Hanuman) loudly calls out, Om, the ancient group of evil trembles.

The verse invokes the power of Hanuman, who is restless in his pursuit of righteousness, to loudly challenge and cause fear in the forces of evil.

॥ बाण चौपाई 31 ॥ Verse 31

अपने जन को कस न उबारौ। सुमिरत होत आनन्द हमारौ॥

apane jana ko kasa na ubāraū। sumirata hota ānanda hamāraū॥

How would you not save your own people? Remembering you brings us joy.

The verse conveys a deep faith in divine intervention and the joy derived from remembering the divine presence in times of distress.

॥ बाण चौपाई 32 ॥ Verse 32

तातें विनती करौं पुकारी। हरहु सकल दुःख विपति हमारी॥

tāteṁ vinatī karauṁ pukārī। harahu sakala duḥkha vipati hamārī॥

Father, I make this plea, calling out to you. Remove all our suffering and calamity.

This verse expresses a deep sense of surrender and faith in the divine. It is a plea for divine intervention to alleviate suffering and hardship.

॥ बाण चौपाई 33 ॥ Verse 33

ऐसौ बल प्रभाव प्रभु तोरा। कस न हरहु दुःख संकट मोरा॥

aisau bala prabhāva prabhu torā। kasa na harahu duḥkha saṅkaṭa morā॥

Such is the power and influence of You, my Lord. Why do You not remove my sorrow and crisis?

The verse emphasizes the immense power of the Lord and expresses the devotee's faith in His ability to alleviate suffering. It is a call for divine intervention in times of hardship.

॥ बाण चौपाई 34 ॥ Verse 34

हे बजरंग, बाण सम धावौ। मेहि सकल दुःख दरस दिखावौ॥

he bajraṅg, bāṇa sama dhāvau। mehi sakala duḥkha darasa dikhāvau॥

O Bajrang, run like an arrow. Show me all the suffering.

The verse is a prayer for divine intervention, asking Hanuman to swiftly come to the devotee's aid and reveal the root of all suffering, so it can be addressed and healed.

॥ बाण चौपाई 35 ॥ Verse 35

हे कपिराज काज कब ऐहौ। अवसर चूकि अन्त पछतैहौ॥

he kapirāja kāja kaba aihau। avasara cūki anta pachatāihau॥

O King of Monkeys, when will you come to do your duty? Missing the opportunity will lead to regret in the end.

This verse is a plea to Hanuman for timely intervention and assistance. It reflects the devotee's fear of missing the divine opportunity and the regret that would follow.

॥ बाण चौपाई 36 ॥ Verse 36

जन की लाज जात ऐहि बारा। धावहु हे कपि पवन कुमारा॥

jana kī lāja jāta aihi bārā। dhāvahu he kapi pavana kumārā॥

The honor of the people is at stake at this time. Run, O monkey, son of the wind.

The verse is a call for divine intervention, specifically from Hanuman, to protect the honor of the people in times of crisis.

॥ बाण चौपाई 37 ॥ Verse 37

जयति जयति जय जय हनुमाना। जयति जयति गुण ज्ञान निधाना॥

jayati jayati jaya jaya hanumānā। jayati jayati guṇa jñāna nidhānā॥

Victory, victory, victory to Hanuman. Victory, victory to the treasure of virtues and knowledge.

The verse is a salutation to Lord Hanuman, acknowledging him as the epitome of virtues and the reservoir of knowledge.

॥ बाण चौपाई 38 ॥ Verse 38

जयति जयति जय जय कपिराई। जयति जयति जय जय सुखदाई॥

jayati jayati jaya jaya kapirāī। jayati jayati jaya jaya sukhadāī॥

Victory, victory, victory to the Monkey Lord. Victory, victory, victory to the Giver of Joy.

This verse is a celebration of Lord Hanuman's power and benevolence, acknowledging him as a source of joy and victory.

॥ बाण चौपाई 39 ॥ Verse 39

जयति जयति जय राम पियारे। जयति जयति जय सिया दुलारे॥

jayati jayati jaya rāma piyāre। jayati jayati jaya siyā dulāre॥

Victory, victory, victory to the beloved Ram. Victory, victory, victory to Sita, the darling.

The verse expresses the devotee's deep love and reverence for Lord Ram and Sita, invoking their blessings and protection.

॥ बाण चौपाई 40 ॥ Verse 40

जयति जयति मुद मंगलदाता। जयति जयति त्रिभुवन विख्याता॥

jaya-ti jaya-ti muda maṅgala-dātā। jaya-ti jaya-ti tri-bhuvana vikh-yātā॥

Triumphs, triumphs the joyous benefactor. Triumphs, triumphs the renowned in three worlds.

The verse celebrates the victory and renown of the joyous benefactor (often interpreted as Lord Hanuman), who is known and revered across all three realms of existence.

॥ बाण चौपाई 41 ॥ Verse 41

ऐहि प्रकार गावत गुण शेषा। पावत पार नहीं लवलेषा॥

aihi prakāra gāvata guṇa śeṣā। pāvata pāra nahīṁ lavalēṣā॥

Singing endless virtues in this manner, one does not attain even a bit beyond.

The verse implies that no matter how much one sings the endless virtues, one cannot fully comprehend or transcend them.

॥ बाण चौपाई 42 ॥ Verse 42

राम रूप सबहि समाना। देखत रहत सदा हर्षाना॥

Rāma rūpa sabahi samānā। Dekhata rahata sadā harṣānā॥

The form of Rama is similar to all. Seeing it always brings joy.

The verse implies that the divine form of Lord Rama is present in all beings and seeing this divine presence always brings joy and happiness.

॥ बाण चौपाई 43 ॥ Verse 43

विधि शारदा सहित दिनराती। गावत कपि के गुण बहु भाँति॥

vidhi śāradā sahita dinarātī। gāvata kapi ke guṇa bahu bhāṁti॥

Brahma and Saraswati, together, day and night, sing the virtues of the Monkey God in many ways.

This verse signifies the immense virtues of Lord Hanuman, which are so great that even the gods Brahma and Saraswati sing his praises day and night.

॥ बाण चौपाई 44 ॥ Verse 44

तुम सम नहीं जगत बलवाना। करि विचार देखउं विधि नाना॥

tum sama nahīṁ jagata balavānā। kari vicāra dekhaūṁ vidhi nānā॥

There is no one in the world as strong as you. I see this after considering various methods.

The verse signifies the unparalleled strength of Lord Hanuman in the universe. It emphasizes that after thorough contemplation and observation, no one matches his power.

॥ बाण चौपाई 45 ॥ Verse 45

यह जानि शरण तब आई। तातें विनय करौं चित लाई॥

yaha jāni śaraṇa taba āī। tāteṁ vinaya karauṁ cita lāī॥

Knowing this, I came to your refuge. Therefore, I bring forth a humble mind.

The verse signifies the importance of humility and surrender in spiritual practice. It emphasizes that true refuge in the divine can only be sought with a humble and sincere heart.

॥ बाण चौपाई 46 ॥ Verse 46

सुनि कपि आरत वचन हमारे। मेटहु सकल दुःख भ्रम भारे॥

suni kapi ārata vacana hamāre। meṭahu sakala duḥkha bhrama bhāre॥

Listen, O Monkey (Hanuman), to our plea. Dissolve all our heavy sufferings and delusions.

The verse is a call to Hanuman, the divine monkey god, to heed the supplicant's pleas and alleviate their sufferings, both physical and mental, and dispel their delusions.

॥ बाण चौपाई 47 ॥ Verse 47

एहि प्रकार विनती कपि केरी। जो जन करै लहै सुख ढेरी॥

ehi prakāra vinatī kapi kerī। jo jana karai lahai sukha ḍherī॥

In this manner, the plea of the monkey god is made. Whoever does this, receives an abundance of happiness.

The verse emphasizes the power of prayer and devotion towards Hanuman. It suggests that sincere devotion and prayer to Hanuman can bring immense happiness and peace.

॥ बाण चौपाई 48 ॥ Verse 48

याके पढ़त वीर हनुमाना। धावत बाण तुल्य बन जाना॥

yāke paḍhata vīra hanumānā। dhāvata bāṇa tulya bana jānā॥

Whoever reads this, brave Hanuman becomes as swift as a running arrow.

The verse signifies the immediate response of Hanuman to the prayers of the devotee. His speed and agility are compared to a swiftly flying arrow.

॥ बाण चौपाई 49 ॥ Verse 49

मेटत आए दुःख क्षण माहीं। देय दर्शन रघुपति ढिग जाहीं॥

meṭata āye duḥkha kṣaṇa māhīṁ। deya darśana raghupati ḍhiga jāhīṁ॥

Eradicates suffering the moment it comes. Gives the vision of Lord Rama, shame go away.

The verse signifies the power of Lord Hanuman to instantly alleviate suffering and bestow the divine vision of Lord Rama, removing any feelings of shame or guilt.

॥ बाण चौपाई 50 ॥ Verse 50

पाठ करें बजरंग बाण की। हनुमत रक्षा करें प्राण की॥

pāṭha kareṁ bajraṅga bāṇa kī। hanumata rakṣā kareṁ prāṇa kī॥

Perform the recitation of Bajrang Baan, Hanuman protects life.

The verse emphasizes the importance of reciting Bajrang Baan for seeking the divine protection of Lord Hanuman over life.

॥ बाण चौपाई 51 ॥ Verse 51

डीन, मूह, होनादिक नासें। परकृत यंत्र मंत्र नहीं नासे॥

ḍīna, mūha, honādika nāseṁ। parkṛta yantra mantra nahīṁ nāse॥

Destroy the distress, fear, etcetera. The natural machines and mantras do not destroy.

The verse calls upon Hanuman to eliminate distress and fear, acknowledging that physical means and mantras alone cannot overcome these obstacles.

॥ बाण चौपाई 52 ॥ Verse 52

भैरवादि सुर करें समताई। आयुस मानि करें सेवकाई॥

Bhairavādi sura kareṁ samatāī। Āyusa māni kareṁ sevakāī॥

Bhairava and other deities perform their duties with equanimity. They consider their life as a service to Hanuman.

The verse suggests that all deities, including Bhairava, perform their duties with equanimity, considering their existence as a service to Lord Hanuman, highlighting his supreme status.

॥ बाण चौपाई 53 ॥ Verse 53

प्रण कर पाठ करें मन लाई। अल्प-मृत्यु ग्रह दोष नसाई॥

praṇa kara pāṭha kareṁ mana lāī। alpa-mṛtyu graha doṣa nasāī॥

Recite with life and mind engaged. It eliminates the faults of the planets and prevents untimely death.

The verse emphasizes the importance of focused and devoted recitation, which has the power to alleviate astrological afflictions and prevent untimely death.

॥ बाण चौपाई 54 ॥ Verse 54

आतुर नियर प्रतिदिन जापें। ताकी छाँव काल नहिं चापें॥

ātur niyar pratidin jāpeṁ। tākī chāṁv kāl nahiṁ chāpeṁ॥

The distressed chant daily so that the shadow of death does not touch them.

This verse suggests that those who are in distress and chant daily are protected from the fear and inevitability of death.

॥ बाण चौपाई 55 ॥ Verse 55

दें गुगुल की धूप हमेशा। करें पाठ तन समहैं कलेषा॥

deṃ gugula kī dhūpa hameśā। kareṃ pāṭha tana samahaiṃ kaleśā॥

Always offer the incense of Guggul. Perform recitations that pacify the afflictions of the body.

The verse suggests the importance of ritualistic offerings and recitations in maintaining spiritual well-being, and in mitigating physical and mental afflictions.

॥ बाण चौपाई 56 ॥ Verse 56

यह बजरंग बाण जेहि मारे। ताहि कहो फिर कौन उबारे॥

yaha bajranga bāṇa jēhi mārē। tāhi kahō phira kauna ubārē॥

Whoever is struck by this Bajranga Baan, who can then save them?

This verse signifies the immense power of Bajranga Baan, suggesting that once it strikes someone, there is no salvation for them.

॥ बाण चौपाई 57 ॥ Verse 57

शत्रु समूह समहैं सब आपें। देखत ताहि सुरासुर काँपें॥

śatru samūha samahaiṁ sab āpeṁ। dekhat tāhi surāsur kām̐pe॥

All groups of enemies themselves, seeing that, gods and demons tremble.

The verse implies the immense power of divine presence, which is so formidable that even the enemies, gods, and demons tremble at its sight.

॥ बाण चौपाई 58 ॥ Verse 58

तेज प्रताप बुद्धि अधिकाई। रहें सदा कपिराज सहाई॥

teja pratāpa buddhi adhikāī। raheṁ sadā kapirāja sahāī॥

Radiance, majesty, superior wisdom, may the King of Monkeys always remain a helper.

The verse is a prayer for the eternal presence and guidance of Hanuman, who embodies radiance, majesty, and superior wisdom.

Concluding Couplet (दोहा)

प्रेम प्रतीतिहिं कपि भजें। सदा धरैं उर ध्यान॥ तेहि के कारज तुरत ही, सिद्ध करें हनुमान॥

prema pratītihiṁ kapi bhajeṁ। sadā dharaiṁ ura dhyāna॥ tehi ke kāraja turata hī, siddha kareṁ hanumāna॥

Those who worship the monkey (Hanuman) with love and belief, always meditate in their heart, their tasks are quickly accomplished by Hanuman.

⚠️ Important Recitation Warning (सावधानी निर्देश)

Unlike the Hanuman Chalisa, which is gentle and can be chanted casually at any time, the **Bajrang Baan** contains seed mantras (bij mantras like hrim, klim, shrim) and involves making a vow (shapath) in the name of Lord Rama to compel Hanuman to act.

Therefore, traditional pandits advise that the Bajrang Baan should **only** be recited when facing severe crisis, extreme fear, or insurmountable difficulties. It should not be chanted for trivial desires or material greed, and the chanter must maintain high physical and moral cleanliness (such as avoiding non-vegetarian food).
