Shri Hanuman Stuti Quick Facts

Deity (देवता) Lord Hanuman / Maruti / Anjaneya (श्री हनुमान)
Language (भाषा) Classical Vedic Sanskrit (संस्कृतम्)
Key Invocations Atulita Bala Dhaamam, Manojavam, Yatra Yatra Raghunatha Keertanam
Usage & Ritual Opening Dhyana before Hanuman Chalisa, Sundarkand, or daily Puja

Hanuman Stuti Verses in English Transliteration & Sanskrit

Recite these timeless stutis to invoke the swift, compassionate energy of Sri Hanuman.

Verse 1: Atulita Bala Dhaamam (अतुलितबलधामं) श्लोक 1: अतुलितबलधामं

अतुलितबलधामं हेमशैलाभदेहं। दनुजवनकृशानुं ज्ञानिनामग्रगण्यम्॥ सकलगुणनिधानं वानराणामधीशं। रघुपतिप्रियभक्तं वातजातं नमामि॥

English Lyrics / Transliteration

Atulita-bala-dhaamam hema-shailaabha-deham | Danuja-vana-krishaanum jnaaninaam-agraganyam || Sakala-guna-nidhaanam vaanaraanaam-adheesham | Raghupati-priya-bhaktam vaatajaatam namaami ||

English Meaning:

I bow to Lord Hanuman, the son of the Wind God (Vatajata), who is the repository of immeasurable strength, whose body gleams like a golden mountain (Mount Sumeru), who is like a blazing fire consuming the forest of demonic forces, who is the foremost among the wise, the treasure-house of all divine virtues, the supreme leader of the Vanaras, and the dearly beloved devotee of Lord Rama.

हिंदी भावार्थ:

अतुलनीय बल के धाम, सुवर्ण पर्वत (सुमेरु) के समान कांतियुक्त शरीर वाले, दैत्य रूपी वन को भस्म करने के लिए अग्नि स्वरूप, ज्ञानियों में अग्रगण्य, संपूर्ण सद्गुणों के निधान, वानरों के स्वामी और श्री रघुनाथ जी के परम प्रिय भक्त पवनपुत्र श्री हनुमान को मैं प्रणाम करता हूँ।

Verse 2: Manojavam Maaruta Tulya Vegam (मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं) श्लोक 2: मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं

मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं। जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम्॥ वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं। श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये॥

English Lyrics / Transliteration

Manojavam maaruta-tulya-vegam | Jitendriyam buddhimataam varishtham || Vaataatmajam vaanara-yootha-mukhyam | Shree-raama-dootam sharanam prapadye ||

English Meaning:

I take refuge in Shri Rama's divine ambassador, Lord Hanuman—who is as swift as thought and endowed with the speed of the rushing wind, who is the master of his senses (Jitendriya), the most eminent among the intelligent, the son of the Wind God, and the supreme commander of the Vanara army.

हिंदी भावार्थ:

मन के समान तीव्र गति और पवन के समान वेग वाले, अपनी सभी इंद्रियों को वश में करने वाले, बुद्धिमानों में सर्वश्रेष्ठ, पवनपुत्र, वानर सेना के प्रमुख और भगवान श्रीराम के पावन दूत श्री हनुमान की मैं शरण लेता हूँ।

Verse 3: Yatra Yatra Raghunatha Keertanam (यत्र यत्र रघुनाथकीर्तनं) श्लोक 3: यत्र यत्र रघुनाथकीर्तनं

यत्र यत्र रघुनाथकीर्तनं। तत्र तत्र कृतमस्तकाञ्जलिम्॥ बाष्पवारिपरिपूर्णलोचनं। मारुतिं नमत राक्षसान्तकम्॥

English Lyrics / Transliteration

Yatra yatra raghunaatha-keertanam | Tatra tatra krita-mastakaanjalim || Baashpa-vaari-paripoorna-lochanam | Maarutim namata raakshasaantakam ||

English Meaning:

Wherever the glories of Lord Raghunatha are sung, there with folded palms placed reverently upon his head and eyes brimming with tears of devotional joy stands Maruti (Hanuman), the terminator of demons. To that glorious Hanuman, I bow in deepest adoration.

हिंदी भावार्थ:

जहाँ-जहाँ भगवान श्री रघुनाथ जी का कीर्तन होता है, वहाँ-वहाँ मस्तक पर हाथ जोड़े हुए, नेत्रों में प्रेमाश्रु भरे हुए, राक्षसों का संहार करने वाले श्री मारुति (हनुमान जी) उपस्थित रहते हैं। ऐसे पावन हनुमान जी को हम सभी प्रणाम करते हैं।

Verse 4: Buddhir Balam Yasho Dhairyam (बुद्धिर्बलं यशोधैर्यं) श्लोक 4: बुद्धिर्बलं यशोधैर्यं

बुद्धिर्बलं यशोधैर्यं। निर्भयत्वमरोगता॥ अजाड्यं वाक्पटुत्वं च। हनूमत्स्मरणाद्भवेत्॥

English Lyrics / Transliteration

Buddhir-balam yasho-dhairyam | Nirbhayatvam-arogataa || Ajaadyam vaak-patutvam cha | Hanoomat-smaranaad-bhavet ||

English Meaning:

Wisdom, strength, good reputation, courage, fearlessness, health, freedom from lethargy, and eloquence of speech—all these eight spiritual and worldly blessings are spontaneously attained simply by remembering Lord Hanuman.

हिंदी भावार्थ:

बुद्धि, शारीरिक बल, पवित्र यश, धैर्य, निर्भयता, रोग-मुक्ति (आरोग्य), चेतना में स्फूर्ति (आलस्यहीनता) और वाणी की कुशलता—ये आठों सिद्धियां केवल श्री हनुमान जी के स्मरण मात्र से प्राप्त हो जाती हैं।

Verse 5: Aanjaneyam Atipaatalaananam (आञ्जनेयमतिपाटलाननं) श्लोक 5: आञ्जनेयमतिपाटलाननं

आञ्जनेयमतिपाटलाननं। काञ्चनाद्रिकमनीयविग्रहम्॥ पारिजाततरुमूलवासिनं। भावयामि पवमाननन्दनम्॥

English Lyrics / Transliteration

Aanjaneyam-ati-paatalaananam | Kaanchanaadri-kamaneeya-vigraham || Paarijaata-taru-moola-vaasinam | Bhaavayaami pavamaana-nandanam ||

English Meaning:

I meditate with intense devotion upon Lord Hanuman, the beloved son of Mother Anjana and the Wind God, whose divine face is delightfully rosy like a fresh crimson flower, whose enchanting body shines like a golden mountain, and who meditates eternally under the shelter of the divine Parijata tree.

हिंदी भावार्थ:

गुलाब के समान सुंदर लाल मुख वाले, स्वर्ण पर्वत के समान मनमोहक देह वाले, कल्पवृक्ष पारिजात के मूल में सदा वास करने वाले माता अंजनी और पवन देव के आनंद स्वरूप श्री हनुमान जी का मैं ध्यान करता हूँ।

Spiritual Significance & The 8 Blessings (अष्ट फल)

In the fourth shloka (Buddhir Balam Yasho Dhairyam), the ancient sages record the eight specific fruits granted by contemplating Lord Hanuman:

1. Buddhi Supreme Intellect
2. Balam Vital Physical Power
3. Yashas Virtuous Renown
4. Dhairyam Unshakable Courage
5. Nirbhayatvam Absolute Fearlessness
6. Arogata Freedom from Disease
7. Ajaadyam Mental Alertness
8. Vaak-Patutvam Eloquence in Speech

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Shri Hanuman Stuti?

Shri Hanuman Stuti is a sacred collection of classical Sanskrit dhyana shlokas (including 'Atulita Bala Dhaamam' and 'Manojavam Maaruta Tulya Vegam') that invoke the divine qualities, speed, intellect, and unconditional surrender of Lord Hanuman.

What are the lyrics of Hanuman Stuti in English?

The foremost verses of the Hanuman Stuti in English transliteration are: 1. 'Atulita-bala-dhaamam hema-shailaabha-deham, danuja-vana-krishaanum jnaaninaam-agraganyam...' 2. 'Manojavam maaruta-tulya-vegam, jitendriyam buddhimataam varishtham...' 3. 'Yatra yatra raghunaatha-keertanam, tatra tatra krita-mastakaanjalim...' The complete 5-verse transliteration and translations are detailed verse-by-verse on this page.

What are the benefits of chanting Manojavam Marutatulyavegam?

Chanting 'Manojavam Maaruta Tulya Vegam' calms the restless mind, brings sensory self-control (Jitendriya), sharpens the intellect (Buddhi), and invokes immediate divine protection in situations of sudden panic, fear, or mental confusion.

When should Hanuman Stuti be recited?

Hanuman Stuti is traditionally chanted prior to reciting the Hanuman Chalisa, Sundarkand, or Ramayana. It is also chanted daily before undertaking important intellectual work, exams, journeys, or meditative practice.
