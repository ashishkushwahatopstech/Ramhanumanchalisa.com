चालीसा चौपाई (प्रारंभिक श्लोक)

श्री हनुमान चालीसा के पवित्र प्रारंभिक श्लोक। संपूर्ण ४० चौपाइयों व शब्दार्थ विश्लेषण हेतु नीचे दिए गए लिंक पर जाएं।

॥ चौपाई 1 ॥पद व्याख्या→

जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर। जय कपीस तिहुं लोक उजागर।।

“Jai Hanuman Gyaan gun saagar Jai Kapis Tihun lok ujagar”

जय हनुमान, ज्ञान और गुणों के सागर। जय वानर-राज, जो तीनों लोकों को प्रकाशित करते हैं।

॥ चौपाई 2 ॥पद व्याख्या→

राम दूत अतुलित बल धामा। अंजनि-पुत्र पवनसुत नामा।।

“Ram doot Atulit bal dhama Anjani-putra Pavansut nama”

आप राम के दूत हैं, अतुलनीय शक्ति के भंडार। आप अंजनी के पुत्र और पवन के पुत्र के रूप में जाने जाते हैं।

॥ चौपाई 3 ॥पद व्याख्या→

महाबीर बिक्रम बजरंगी। कुमति निवार सुमति के संगी।।

“Mahavir Bikram bajrangi Kumati nivar Sumati ke sangi”

हे महान वीर, अपार पराक्रम वाले, वज्र के समान मजबूत शरीर वाले, आप दुर्विचारों को दूर करते हैं और सद्विचारों के साथी हैं।

Frequently Asked Questions

हनुमान चालीसा किसने लिखी थी? (हनुमान चालीसा किसने लिखी / किसने लिखा था)

हनुमान चालीसा की रचना 16वीं शताब्दी में महान कवि गोस्वामी तुलसीदास जी ने की थी।

हनुमान चालीसा पढ़ने से क्या होता है?

हनुमान चालीसा का नियमित पाठ करने से सभी प्रकार के संकट, रोग, और भय दूर हो जाते हैं। यह साधक को मानसिक दृढ़ता, सुख-शांति, और शनिदेव (Saturn) के दुष्प्रभावों से सुरक्षा प्रदान करती है।

हनुमान चालीसा के क्या लाभ हैं?

हनुमान चालीसा के नियमित पाठ से आत्मविश्वास बढ़ता है, सभी भय दूर होते हैं, और जीवन की बाधाएं मिटती हैं।

Understanding Shree Hanuman Chalisa: Origin, Significance & Recitation

An authentic guide for devotees to explore the history, lyrical architecture, spiritual benefits, and sacred chanting discipline of Goswami Tulsidas's immortal Awadhi hymn.

॥ What is the Hanuman Chalisa? (परिचय एवं इतिहास)

The Shree Hanuman Chalisa is a revered forty-verse devotional stotra composed in the 16th century by the celebrated Bhakti saint and poet Goswami Tulsidas (1532–1623 CE). Composed in melodic Awadhi—an Eastern Hindi literary language closely linked with Sanskrit—the word Chalisa derives from the Hindi word chalis, signifying forty. The hymn consists of two introductory couplets (dohas), forty rhythmic quatrains (chaupais), and a concluding blessing doha.

According to devotional tradition, Tulsidas composed this sacred prayer while imprisoned in Fatehpur Sikri by the Mughal Emperor Akbar after refusing to perform an imperial miracle. Turning his complete surrender toward Sri Hanuman—the guardian deity of all Rama bhaktas—Tulsidas meditated upon Hanumanji's divine attributes, valor, and humble servitude. On the fortieth day of recitation, an extraordinary swarm of monkeys entered the citadel, leading to the saint's honorable release and immortalizing the forty chaupais as a protective armor for humanity.

॥ Spiritual & Mental Benefits of Daily Chanting (फलश्रुति)

Devotees across the world recite the Hanuman Chalisa daily to invoke spiritual courage, dispel fear, and invite auspicious blessings into their homes. Classical commentaries emphasize that chanting these sacred Awadhi verses produces harmonizing vibrations that calm nervous tension, dissolve anxiety, and awaken inner fortitude.

Scriptures specifically highlight that sincere contemplation of Mahavira Hanuman mitigates the adverse influences of planetary transitions, especially Saturn's Shani Sade Sati and Dhayya. Chaupai 25 proclaims “Nasai rog harai sab peera, japat nirantar Hanumat beera”, assuring seekers that continuous recitation acts as a powerful healing balm for chronic distress and mental exhaustion. To learn how individual chaupais address specific life situations including health, career hurdles, and studies, explore our comprehensive Hanuman Chalisa Benefits Guide.

॥ How to Recite: Path Vidhi, Timing & Rules (शास्त्रोक्त पाठ विधि)

To receive the maximum spiritual resonance from your daily recitation, tradition recommends reciting during Brahma Muhurat (approximately 4:00 AM to 5:30 AM before sunrise) or during evening Sandhya hours at dusk. Devotees should cleanse themselves with a bath, sit facing East or North on a clean wool or kusha grass mat (aasan), and illuminate a brass diya with pure cow ghee or sesame (til) oil.

It is auspicious to begin every chanting session by remembering Bhagwan Shri Ram Chandra and Mother Sita, as Lord Hanuman is perpetually present wherever the holy name of Rama is uttered with devotion. Depending on one's daily vows (sankalpa), the Chalisa may be chanted 1, 3, 7, 11, or 100 times (Shatpath). For detailed rules on pronunciation, sankalpa guidelines, Tuesday-Saturday fasting, and deity offerings, refer to our complete Hanuman Chalisa Path Vidhi Guide.
